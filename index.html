                        How to read the dates

                        A chronology with honest seams

                        Ovid announces a continuous song “from the world’s first origin down to my own time,” but his order is literary before it is historical. Stories nest inside stories; narrators look backward; thematic echoes can matter more than sequence.

                        This companion therefore uses broad eras and traditional anchors. Dates before Troy are visualization aids. The wheel keeps an approximation mark beside every synthetic date and lets the poem’s own book order remain primary.
